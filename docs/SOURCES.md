## SciFact material in the logs

The claim text and judgment identifiers in `results/`, and the claim excerpts in the presentation, come from SciFact by Wadden et al., using the pinned BEIR distribution above. The experiment selects positive test judgments and associates each selected claim with the ranking and measurements generated by this study. We retain the BEIR distribution's CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution for the embedded data. The original SciFact license lists CC BY 4.0 for claims and evidence annotations and ODC-By 1.0 for abstracts.
